Refinement-DAG Software Development

A Calculus of Verified Behavior Inclusions for Agent-Authored Systems

The development method serves five root purposes (P1–P5): realizing committed behavior, preserving authority over governing meaning, keeping justified change local, reducing human governance context, and keeping assurance accountable to evidence. Its compositional theorems relate local component machines, parent-owned contracts, executable leaves, and architectural connectors through finite visible-trace inclusion under three operational disciplines (O1–O3), paired with separate operational obligations for reception, readiness, and contract-relative non-silent response.
